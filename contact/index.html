How can we help you?

Advertising — Reach Arizona's equestrian community through Bridle & Bit Magazine and our network of 20+ digital properties with 60,000+ Facebook community members. We offer print, digital, and social placement. Tell us about your business and we'll put together a media package.

Sponsorship — Partner with Bridle & Bit to sponsor events, arena listings, video series, or our AI platforms including TeamRoping.AI, HorseTrainer.AI, and more. Sponsorships drive year-round brand visibility across print, digital, and social channels.

Editorial — Submit a story idea, press release, event announcement, or photo for consideration. We cover team roping, reining, reined cow horse, barrel racing, rodeo, western lifestyle, and Arizona equestrian news. Include as much detail as possible.

Support — Questions about our websites, AI platforms, Facebook groups, or digital properties? Let us know what you're experiencing and we'll get back to you promptly.

Submit Your Flyer to the Calendar — Send us your event flyer or image to be considered for HorseCalendar.ai. Fill in the form below and use Add Your Flyer or Image to choose your file (JPG, PNG or PDF). Please include the event name, dates, location, entry fees and a contact.

Calendar & Print Policies

  • What we list: equestrian and western events such as horse shows, rodeos, ropings, barrel racing, bull riding, cutting, driving, gymkhana, ranch sorting, horse sales and auctions, trail rides, fairs and clinics. Events with no horse content are not listed automatically; we will check with you first.
  • What to send: your flyer or image (JPG, PNG or PDF) with the event name, dates, venue, city and state, entry fees and a contact.
  • Incomplete flyers: if your flyer is missing dates, fees or class details, we may list the event with the information available and a "Watch for More Info!" note rather than guess at details.
  • Updates: if you send a newer flyer for the same event, we update the listing to match it.
  • Review: every submission is reviewed by our staff, and listing is not guaranteed.
  • Print calendar: tick the print option below to have your event considered for the Bridle & Bit Magazine print calendar, which covers Arizona and Las Vegas events, plus horse auctions nationwide. Print listings are edited for length, and placement is at the editors' discretion.

General Inquiries — Subscriptions, letters to the editor, community questions, or anything else. We've been serving Arizona's equestrian community since 1978 and love hearing from our readers.
